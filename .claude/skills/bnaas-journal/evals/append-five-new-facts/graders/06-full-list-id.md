The outline read printed the Log's list id in its short form (such as `.1408`), and the append names that list by its full id, the session prefix and the clock (such as `m1yth80jczv.1408`), never the short form; no update call is refused for its target id. If the outline printed the list's id in full, the precondition was not met (the list must print short, as in the real Log), and this grader fails.
